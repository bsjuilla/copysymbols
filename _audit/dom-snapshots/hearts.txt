Frequently Asked Questions
How do I type a heart symbol?

The easiest way is to click any heart on this page — it copies to your clipboard instantly. You can then paste it anywhere. On a Mac, you can also press Option+V for ♥. On Windows, hold Alt and type 3 on the numpad.

What is the difference between ❤ and ♥?

❤ is the emoji version of the heart, which displays in colour on modern devices. ♥ is the Unicode heart suit character from card games, which often displays in black. Both copy and paste the same way.

Which heart symbol works on Instagram?

All the hearts on this page work on Instagram. The most popular for Instagram bios are ❤, ♡, 💕, and 🖤. For a minimalist look, ♡ (the outline heart) is very popular.

How do I make a heart on my keyboard?

There is no dedicated heart key on most keyboards. The fastest way is to copy from this page. Alternatively, on Mac use Option+V for ♥, on Windows use Alt+3 (numpad) for ♥, or on any phone open the emoji keyboard.
